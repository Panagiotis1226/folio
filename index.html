02 / approach

Three rules I work by

  1. i.

    Start with the chore

    The best thing to automate is rarely the interesting thing. It’s the task someone does the same way every day. I go looking for hours, not novelty.

  2. ii.

    Use what’s already there

    No new platform for the team to learn. I wire AI into the tools they already live in — Shopify, Gorgias, Cin7, ShipStation, Excel.

  3. iii.

    Write it down

    Every automation ships with an SOP, so it keeps working — and someone else can maintain it — after I’ve moved on to the next one.

04 / about

From the support queue to the systems behind it

I’m a computer science graduate in Montréal who likes the unglamorous half of technology: the part where it has to keep running.

I joined Polysleep in customer service and moved into business systems and operations within months. Today I own the daily sales and customer-experience reporting, run batch order fulfillment across Cin7 and ShipStation, and lead the team’s AI adoption.

Before that I led people — coached soccer teams, ran a summer camp’s operations, promoted events — which is why I measure work by whether it got easier for whoever has to do it. Along the way I also made short-form video for Reels, TikTok, and Shorts.
